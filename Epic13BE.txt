Epic 13 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 13 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 13 title

Goals, tasks, and care progression

Epic 13 feature scope from the requirements

- goal templates
- interventions/tasks
- target dates
- completed / unmet / not attained states
- progress notes
- version history
- care-plan sync

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records and patient-scoped clinical context that goals and interventions attach to
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit and scheduling context that may reference interventions or completion progress
- Epic 8 is complete and provides documentation templates, task completion, and progress-note-adjacent capture patterns that Epic 13 can reuse without duplicating
- Epic 11 is complete and provides compliance and care-plan completeness context that may consume care-goal state later but should not redefine goal progression here
- Epic 12 is complete and provides incident/infection/wound workflows that may influence care progression but should remain distinct Epic 12 records
- Epic 13 focuses on goal templates, patient-specific goals, interventions/tasks, target dates, outcome states, progress notes, version history, and care-plan synchronization, not revenue workflow or physician-order workflow from later epics
- goal and intervention workflows should be simpler and more operationally usable than legacy enterprise screens while still preserving history and auditability
- care-plan sync should support durable linkage between patient care-plan state and goal/intervention records rather than fragile frontend-only composition

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 13 backend work
- Cover goal templates, patient goal records, intervention/task records, target-date tracking, progression states, progress notes, version history, care-plan synchronization, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull wound workflows, generalized documentation engines, or billing workflows from other epics into Epic 13

Definition of done for Epic 13 backend

Epic 13 backend is done only when:

- goal-template, patient-goal, intervention, progress-note, version-history, care-plan-sync, and progression-projection domain models exist with clear lifecycle rules
- goal, intervention, progress-note, and progression-summary APIs are available for real operational use
- target-date, state-transition, and care-plan-sync actions are implemented through audited APIs
- completed, unmet, not-attained, and overdue progression outcomes are available through structured backend contracts
- role-based authorization and tenant or branch-aware privacy rules are enforced for patient care-goal data
- longitudinal goal history and care-plan-sync state are queryable through backend APIs suitable for operational review
- progression state is derived deterministically from source records rather than ad hoc frontend logic
- core Epic 13 workflows are regression-covered
