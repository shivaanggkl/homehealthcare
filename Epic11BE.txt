Epic 11 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 11 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 11 title

Clinical compliance workspace

Epic 11 feature scope from the requirements

- care-plan completeness checklist
- required documentation tracking
- consent/right-to-care acknowledgments
- certification period awareness
- patient risk reminders
- compliance status dashboard

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records, service context, payer context, and patient-scoped lifecycle data needed for compliance evaluation
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit and schedule data that compliance tracking may reference for documentation and follow-up timing
- Epic 7 is complete and provides EVV, signature, and visit-exception outputs that can contribute to compliance posture but should not be re-modeled here
- Epic 8 is complete and provides documentation templates, visit documentation records, required-field enforcement, printable summaries, and attachment linkage that are central to compliance evaluation
- Epic 10 is complete and provides QA/review queue foundations, but Epic 11 should focus on clinical compliance state and checklist evaluation rather than duplicating general QA workflow
- Epic 11 focuses on internal compliance readiness and documentation completeness, not full physician-order workflow, claims workflow, or Medicare-heavy enterprise billing depth from later epics
- care-plan and compliance tracking should be explicitly model-driven and configurable enough for branch-aware maturity without becoming a fully generic rules engine in the first pass
- certification-period awareness should support operational visibility and validation, even if deeper skilled-home-health regulatory coverage evolves later

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 11 backend work
- Cover compliance domain models, checklist and required-document evaluation, consent acknowledgment tracking, certification-period awareness, patient risk reminders, compliance dashboard projections, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull QA review queue, incident/infection/wound workflows, goals engine, or billing workflow scope from other epics into Epic 11

Definition of done for Epic 11 backend

Epic 11 backend is done only when:

- care-plan checklist, required-documentation, acknowledgment, certification-period, risk-reminder, and compliance-projection domain models exist with clear lifecycle rules
- compliance dashboard and patient-compliance APIs are available for real operational queries
- acknowledgment, certification, risk-reminder, and recalculation actions are implemented through audited APIs
- compliance gaps, checklist results, and risk reminders are available through structured backend contracts
- role-based authorization and tenant or branch-aware privacy rules are enforced for compliance data
- compliance history and audit context are queryable through backend APIs suitable for operational review
- compliance projection is deterministic and explicitly derived from source records rather than ad hoc frontend logic
- core Epic 11 compliance flows are regression-covered
