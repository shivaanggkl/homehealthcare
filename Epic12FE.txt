Epic 12 Frontend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 12 from `Requirements.txt` into a frontend delivery backlog for incident, infection, and wound workflows.

Epic 12 title

Incident, infection, and wound workflows

Epic 12 feature scope from the requirements

- incident logging
- infection log
- wound record
- attachments/photos
- follow-up owner
- due dates
- escalation and alerts
- longitudinal history

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 frontend and backend are complete and provide auth, session, RBAC, protected-route, audit-view, and attachment-aware patterns
- Epic 3 frontend and backend are complete and provide patient workspaces and patient context that Epic 12 screens will extend
- Epic 5 frontend and backend are complete and provide visit context that incidents and follow-up work may link back into
- Epic 6 and Epic 7 frontend and backend are complete and provide mobile capture, field artifacts, incident-reporting patterns, and EVV exception context that Epic 12 can build on
- Epic 8 frontend and backend are complete and provide documentation and attachment-link patterns that Epic 12 may reuse for evidence handling
- Epic 9 frontend and backend are complete and provide secure coordination patterns that may be linked for follow-up without duplicating messaging inside Epic 12
- Epic 12 frontend should support structured patient-event workflows and longitudinal visibility, not the goal progression workspace from Epic 13 or revenue workflow from Epic 14
- Epic 12 screens should stay privacy-aware and show only the minimum patient, visit, and evidence detail needed for operational and clinical follow-up

Status legend

- BLOCKED: frontend should wait for the corresponding Epic 12 backend APIs

========================================================================
FE12-01 — Patient-event workspace information architecture and route model
Related Epic 12 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As a reviewer, coordinator, or clinician, I want incident, infection, and wound routes to fit naturally into the app so patient-event work is easy to find and manage.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- protected patient-event workspace route exists
- dedicated routes exist for:
  - incident list/detail
  - infection log/detail
  - wound list/detail
  - patient event timeline
  - follow-up or escalation visibility
- unsupported or unauthorized Epic 12 routes render controlled states
- routes can link back into related patient, visit, documentation, and messaging context where authorized

FE12-10 — Shared mutation and follow-up workflow UX
Related Epic 12 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As the frontend team, I want one mutation pattern for create, update, resolve, follow-up, and escalation actions so Epic 12 behavior stays predictable and safe.

Definition of done for Epic 12 frontend

Epic 12 frontend is done only when:

- incident, infection, wound, evidence, follow-up, and patient-timeline screens are reachable through coherent protected routes
- incident, infection, wound, evidence, follow-up, and escalation flows use real backend APIs rather than placeholder local state
- longitudinal patient-event history is shown clearly and consistently
- coordinator-facing patient-event visibility exists for authorized users
- privacy-sensitive patient and evidence context is minimized appropriately
- Epic 12 screens are usable on smaller operational devices
- core Epic 12 screens are regression-covered
