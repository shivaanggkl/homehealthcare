Epic 5 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 5 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 5 title

Scheduling engine

Epic 5 feature scope from the requirements

- day/week/month schedule views
- drag-and-drop assignment
- caregiver-patient matching
- conflict detection
- overtime and overlap checks
- travel-time awareness
- recurring visits
- open-shift pool
- rescheduling workflow
- cancellation workflow

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides security, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch scoping, and session foundations
- Epic 2 is complete and provides reusable service-line, visit-type, branch-policy, alert-rule, and compensation configuration
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records, payer/authorization context, and patient attachment patterns
- Epic 4 is complete and provides caregiver workforce records, availability, unavailability, geography, and performance inputs
- Epic 5 is an admin and coordinator scheduling engine, not a field-execution or EVV module
- Epic 5 should stop short of mobile visit execution, GPS proof, signatures, and clock-in/out because those belong to Epics 6 and 7
- schedule decisions are always agency-scoped and branch-aware when the selected patient, caregiver, or policy makes branch scoping relevant
- scheduling should produce explicit visit, assignment, and exception records instead of implicit UI-only state

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 5 backend work
- Cover domain model, rule evaluation, authorization, audit, public APIs, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull mobile execution, EVV, visit-note completion, or billing logic from later epics into Epic 5
