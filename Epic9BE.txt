Epic 9 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 9 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 9 title

Messaging and coordination

Epic 9 feature scope from the requirements

- in-app secure messaging
- patient-level communication threads
- staff groups
- branch broadcasts
- visit-specific notes
- escalation tags
- read receipts
- task-linked discussion threads

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records and patient-scoped context for communication threads
- Epic 4 is complete and provides caregiver workforce records and staff identity context
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit, assignment, schedule, and task-adjacent coordination context
- Epic 6 is complete and provides mobile messaging patterns, caregiver-facing message delivery expectations, and offline-tolerant mobile execution assumptions
- Epic 8 is complete and provides visit documentation and task-completion context that messaging threads may reference but should not duplicate
- Epic 9 focuses on secure operational messaging and coordination, not QA review queues from Epic 10, broader compliance workflows from Epic 11, or incident/wound longitudinal workflows from Epic 12
- Epic 9 should support both direct person-to-person coordination and structured context-linked conversations without becoming a full external email or SMS platform
- branch broadcasts should stay agency-internal and role-aware

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 9 backend work
- Cover secure thread/message models, recipients, staff groups, branch broadcasts, visit/patient/task linkage, escalation tags, read receipts, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull QA queue, generalized notifications center, incident workflow, or orders workflow scope from later epics into Epic 9

Related Epic 9 features: all
Current status: NOT STARTED

Story
As the platform architect, I need a coherent messaging domain so secure threads, broadcasts, escalation signals, and contextual coordination all follow one lifecycle and authorization model.

  - communication thread
  - communication message
  - thread participant
  - staff group
  - branch broadcast
  - read receipt

  - direct secure message
  - patient-level thread
  - visit-level thread
  - task-linked discussion
  - branch broadcast
- ownership and linkage rules are documented across agency, branch, patient, visit, task context, and staff participants

- audit expectations are defined for thread creation, message send, participant changes, broadcasts, escalation tagging, and read state updates
