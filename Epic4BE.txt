Epic 4 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 4 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 4 title

Caregiver workforce management

Epic 4 feature scope from the requirements

- caregiver profile
- credentials / licensure
- availability
- preferred geography
- language
- skills
- shift preferences
- PTO / unavailability
- performance indicators

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and available as the security, tenancy, RBAC, audit, and session foundation
- Epic 2 is complete and provides reusable agency setup data such as service lines, visit types, skills, certifications, and branch policies
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient and attachment patterns, but Epic 4 remains workforce-scoped rather than patient-scoped
- caregiver workforce records are agency-scoped, with branch association supported where the operating model needs branch-limited assignment or visibility
- Epic 4 should stop short of full scheduling-board logic, route optimization, or visit assignment automation because those belong to Epic 5
- performance indicators in Epic 4 are operational rollups and profile-visible metrics, not full analytics warehousing

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 4 backend work
- Cover domain model, validation, authorization, audit, public APIs, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull scheduling-engine logic from Epic 5 into Epic 4

Definition of done for Epic 4 backend

Epic 4 backend is done only when:

- caregiver workforce records are modeled separately from base user accounts and can be maintained through public APIs
- credentials, languages, skills, geography preferences, shift preferences, availability, and PTO or unavailability are represented through auditable backend entities
- Epic 4 APIs are permission-aware and compatible with branch-scoped access rules
- overlap, duplicate, and invalid-window conflicts are returned as controlled errors
- performance indicators are available through a defined backend contract
- workforce changes are audit-visible
- Epic 4 backend regression coverage exists for representative CRUD, denial, and conflict behavior
