Epic 4 Frontend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 4 from `Requirements.txt` into a frontend delivery backlog for the web application.

Epic 4 title

Caregiver workforce management

Epic 4 feature scope from the requirements

- caregiver profile
- credentials / licensure
- availability
- preferred geography
- language
- skills
- shift preferences
- PTO / unavailability
- performance indicators

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 frontend and backend are complete and provide the app shell, permissions, navigation, and audit viewer
- Epic 2 setup data is available and reusable where Epic 4 screens need branch data, skills, certifications, or service-line context
- Epic 3 patient workflows are complete, but Epic 4 remains workforce-scoped rather than scheduling-board-scoped
- Epic 4 is primarily an admin and coordinator web experience in this phase
- Epic 4 should not absorb drag-and-drop scheduling or visit-assignment behavior because those belong to Epic 5
- workforce performance indicators in Epic 4 are profile and directory surfaces, not full dashboard analytics

Status legend

- BLOCKED: frontend should wait for the corresponding Epic 4 backend APIs

FE4-01 — Workforce workspace navigation and information architecture
Related Epic 4 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As an agency user, I want a coherent caregiver workforce area in the app so staff records are easy to find and manage.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- a dedicated caregiver or workforce section exists in navigation
- routes are grouped by workforce management domain
- role-based navigation visibility follows backend permissions
- unauthorized deep links render controlled `403` states
- workforce screens share consistent list, detail, and form patterns

Definition of done for Epic 4 frontend

Epic 4 frontend is done only when:

- caregiver records are reachable from the app shell through a dedicated workforce workspace
- Epic 4 screens use backend APIs rather than placeholder local state
- create/edit flows for caregiver profile, credentials, languages, skills, geography, shift preferences, availability, PTO or unavailability, and performance views are usable and permission-aware
- conflict, validation, and unauthorized errors are shown clearly
- caregiver detail navigation is coherent and reusable
- audit-sensitive workforce changes are reflected in the UI
- core Epic 4 screens are regression-covered
