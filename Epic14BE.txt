Epic 14 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 14 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 14 title

Revenue readiness and basic financial ops

Epic 14 feature scope from the requirements

Early version

- visit completion validation
- signed visit validation
- exception flags
- export-ready invoices/payroll data
- authorization usage
- payer/service summary

Later version, intentionally out of scope for this backlog

- claim generation
- physician signature tracking
- denial worklists
- remittance posting

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, exportability foundations, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records, payer linkage, authorization data, and patient service context needed for revenue-readiness checks
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit, assignment, open-shift, reschedule, and cancellation records that form the operational revenue-readiness input
- Epic 7 is complete and provides EVV, signature verification, missed-visit, and exception outputs that contribute to signed-visit and completion readiness
- Epic 8 is complete and provides visit documentation records, submission state, printable summaries, and required-field enforcement that contribute to completion readiness
- Epic 10 and Epic 11 are complete and provide QA/compliance review and documentation completeness context that may feed readiness signals but should not be re-modeled here
- Epic 14 focuses on upstream revenue readiness and exportable operational finance outputs, not full claims processing, remittance workflows, or enterprise revenue-cycle automation
- revenue readiness should detect operational blockers before billing rather than introducing a full billing system in the first pass
- export-ready output should support agency operational handoff without assuming payer-integrated claim submission in scope

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 14 backend work
- Cover readiness validation, signed-visit validation, exception flags, export-ready payroll/invoice output, authorization usage tracking, payer/service summaries, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull later claim-generation, denial, remittance, or physician-order workflow scope into the early Epic 14 backlog

Definition of done for Epic 14 backend

Epic 14 backend is done only when:

- readiness validation, signature validation, revenue exception, export output, authorization-usage, and payer/service summary domain models or projections exist with clear lifecycle rules
- revenue-readiness, exception, export, and authorization-usage APIs are available for real operational use
- readiness recalculation and export actions are implemented through audited APIs
- ready/blocked/warning outcomes are available through structured backend contracts
- role-based authorization and tenant or branch-aware privacy rules are enforced for operational finance data
- export-ready invoice and payroll output is generated safely without assuming full claims processing in scope
- readiness and handoff history are queryable through backend APIs suitable for operational review
- core Epic 14 early-version workflows are regression-covered
