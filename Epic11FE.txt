Epic 11 Frontend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 11 from `Requirements.txt` into a frontend delivery backlog for the clinical compliance workspace.

Epic 11 title

Clinical compliance workspace

Epic 11 feature scope from the requirements

- care-plan completeness checklist
- required documentation tracking
- consent/right-to-care acknowledgments
- certification period awareness
- patient risk reminders
- compliance status dashboard

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 frontend and backend are complete and provide auth, session, RBAC, protected-route, and audit-view patterns
- Epic 3 frontend and backend are complete and provide patient workspaces and patient context that compliance screens may reference
- Epic 5 frontend and backend are complete and provide visit and scheduling context that compliance screens may link back into where appropriate
- Epic 7 frontend and backend are complete and provide EVV, signature, and visit-exception context that may inform compliance state
- Epic 8 frontend and backend are complete and provide documentation records, required-field enforcement, printable summaries, and attachments that form a major part of compliance evaluation
- Epic 10 frontend and backend are complete and provide QA/review patterns, but Epic 11 should focus on clinical compliance visibility and management rather than duplicating the generic QA queue
- Epic 11 frontend should support focused compliance operations and dashboard visibility, not the incident/wound longitudinal workspace from Epic 12 or revenue-readiness workflow from Epic 14
- compliance screens should stay privacy-aware and show only the minimum patient, visit, and documentation detail needed for operational compliance work

Status legend

- BLOCKED: frontend should wait for the corresponding Epic 11 backend APIs

========================================================================
FE11-01 — Compliance workspace information architecture and route model
Related Epic 11 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As a compliance lead or clinical reviewer, I want the compliance workspace routes to fit naturally into the app so dashboard, patient detail, reminders, and checklist work are easy to find.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- protected compliance workspace route exists
- dedicated routes exist for:
  - compliance dashboard
  - patient compliance detail
  - checklist and documentation-gap detail
  - acknowledgment management
  - certification-period management
  - risk-reminder management
- unsupported or unauthorized compliance routes render controlled states
- compliance routes can link back into related patient, documentation, QA, EVV, and scheduling context where authorized

Related Epic 11 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Related Epic 11 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story

Definition of done for Epic 11 frontend

Epic 11 frontend is done only when:

- compliance dashboard, patient detail, checklist, acknowledgment, certification, and risk-reminder screens are reachable through coherent protected routes
- dashboard, checklist, acknowledgment, certification, reminder, and recalculation flows use real backend APIs rather than placeholder local state
- compliance gaps, checklist results, certification state, and risk reminders are shown clearly and consistently
- coordinator-facing compliance visibility exists for authorized users
- privacy-sensitive patient and documentation context is minimized appropriately
- compliance screens are usable on smaller operational devices
- core Epic 11 screens are regression-covered
