Epic 12 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 12 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 12 title

Incident, infection, and wound workflows

Epic 12 feature scope from the requirements

- incident logging
- infection log
- wound record
- attachments/photos
- follow-up owner
- due dates
- escalation and alerts
- longitudinal history

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, attachment-storage foundations, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records, contact context, and patient-scoped history surfaces that incident, infection, and wound workflows can link to
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit and scheduling context that incidents and follow-up work may reference
- Epic 6 and Epic 7 are complete and provide mobile capture, field artifacts, incidents, EVV exceptions, and signature context that Epic 12 can extend into structured longitudinal workflows
- Epic 8 is complete and provides documentation records and attachment linkage that may provide supporting evidence for incidents and wound history
- Epic 9 is complete and provides secure coordination patterns that Epic 12 may reference for escalation/follow-up without duplicating messaging
- Epic 11 is complete and provides compliance/risk reminder patterns that may consume Epic 12 outputs later but should not redefine them here
- Epic 12 focuses on structured operational and clinical event tracking with follow-up ownership and history, not the care-goal progression engine from Epic 13 and not revenue-readiness workflows from Epic 14
- wound and infection workflows should support longitudinal patient history rather than being treated as one-off notes or message threads

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 12 backend work
- Cover incident, infection, wound, attachment linkage, follow-up ownership, due dates, escalation/alert state, longitudinal-history models, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull generalized messaging, goals/interventions, or billing workflows from other epics into Epic 12

Definition of done for Epic 12 backend

Epic 12 backend is done only when:

- incident, infection, wound, evidence, follow-up, escalation, and longitudinal-history domain models exist with clear lifecycle rules
- incident, infection, wound, and patient-timeline APIs are available for real operational use
- follow-up ownership, due-date, evidence, and escalation actions are implemented through audited APIs
- longitudinal patient event history is available through structured backend contracts
- role-based authorization and tenant or branch-aware privacy rules are enforced for sensitive patient event data
- attachments/photos are linked safely without exposing storage internals
- patient event history and alert context are queryable through backend APIs suitable for operational review
- core Epic 12 workflows are regression-covered
