Epic 13 Frontend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 13 from `Requirements.txt` into a frontend delivery backlog for goals, tasks, and care progression.

Epic 13 title

Goals, tasks, and care progression

Epic 13 feature scope from the requirements

- goal templates
- interventions/tasks
- target dates
- completed / unmet / not attained states
- progress notes
- version history
- care-plan sync

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 frontend and backend are complete and provide auth, session, RBAC, protected-route, and audit-view patterns
- Epic 3 frontend and backend are complete and provide patient workspaces and patient context that Epic 13 screens will extend
- Epic 5 frontend and backend are complete and provide visit context that may link into intervention/task progress
- Epic 8 frontend and backend are complete and provide documentation and task-entry patterns that Epic 13 can reuse without duplicating generic documentation workflows
- Epic 11 frontend and backend are complete and provide compliance context that may consume care-goal state later but should not redefine care progression here
- Epic 12 frontend and backend are complete and provide patient-event context that may influence care progression but remains distinct from goals/progression workflow
- Epic 13 frontend should support simpler, more usable progression workflows than legacy enterprise screens while preserving history and operational clarity
- Epic 13 screens should stay privacy-aware and show only the minimum patient and care-plan context needed for goal/progression work

Status legend

- BLOCKED: frontend should wait for the corresponding Epic 13 backend APIs

========================================================================
FE13-01 — Goal workspace information architecture and route model
Related Epic 13 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As a clinician, reviewer, or operations lead, I want goal and progression routes to fit naturally into the app so templates, patient goals, interventions, and history are easy to find.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- protected goal workspace route exists
- dedicated routes exist for:
  - goal-template management
  - patient goal list/detail
  - intervention and task detail where appropriate
  - progress-note and version-history visibility
  - care-plan sync visibility
- unsupported or unauthorized Epic 13 routes render controlled states
- routes can link back into related patient, visit, documentation, and compliance context where authorized

FE13-10 — Shared progression mutation UX
Related Epic 13 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As the frontend team, I want one mutation pattern for templates, goals, interventions, progress notes, state changes, and sync actions so Epic 13 behavior stays predictable and safe.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- progression mutations use a consistent pending/success/failure pattern
- retry behavior is explicit for recoverable failures
- high-impact actions such as state changes or sync updates are confirmed clearly where appropriate
- refreshed list/detail/history state is shown after backend success
- failed mutations remain understandable and retryable where practical

Definition of done for Epic 13 frontend

Epic 13 frontend is done only when:

- goal-template, patient-goal, intervention, progress-note, version-history, and care-plan-sync screens are reachable through coherent protected routes
- template, goal, intervention, progress-note, target-date, state-change, and sync flows use real backend APIs rather than placeholder local state
- completed, unmet, not-attained, overdue, and sync-state outcomes are shown clearly and consistently
- coordinator-facing progression visibility exists for authorized users
- privacy-sensitive patient and care-plan context is minimized appropriately
- Epic 13 screens are usable on smaller operational devices
- core Epic 13 screens are regression-covered
