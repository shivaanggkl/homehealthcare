Epic 7 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 7 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 7 title

Visit execution and EVV

Epic 7 feature scope from the requirements

- clock-in / clock-out
- GPS + timestamp verification
- device metadata
- geofence tolerance rules
- patient/caregiver signature
- missed-visit workflow
- exception reason capture
- supervisor notification
- order/escalation creation on exceptions

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records, addresses, contacts, and patient-level context needed for visit verification and missed-visit follow-up
- Epic 4 is complete and provides caregiver profiles and branch-aware workforce context
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit, assignment, schedule status, open-shift, and recurrence models
- Epic 6 is complete and provides caregiver mobile execution, start/end visit sessions, field artifact support, message center, and offline sync contracts
- Epic 7 extends Epic 6 from general mobile execution into compliance-sensitive EVV and exception workflows
- Epic 7 should not pull in the broader structured notes/forms scope of Epic 8 or the generalized messaging scope of Epic 9
- Epic 7 should not implement the later QA/review workspace of Epic 10 beyond creating the statuses, exception records, and hooks that Epic 10 will consume
- geofence and visit-verification rules should be configurable enough for real agencies without becoming a full external maps/routing product

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 7 backend work
- Cover EVV verification, location/time/device proof, geofence evaluation, exception/missed-visit workflows, notifications, escalation hooks, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull full note-template/form, generalized coordination/messaging, QA queue workspace, or billing workflow scope from later epics into Epic 7

Related Epic 7 features: all
Current status: NOT STARTED

Story

  - EVV verification session or aggregate

  - signature verification artifact linkage
  - missed-visit record

  - pending_verification
  - verified
  - verified_with_warning
  - exception_open
  - exception_acknowledged
  - missed_visit_reported
  - escalated
  - resolved
- ownership and linkage rules are documented across visit, caregiver, patient, branch, and schedule assignment
- extension points for Epic 10 QA review and Epic 12 revenue-readiness workflows are documented without implementing those later-epic workspaces
- audit expectations are documented for every EVV-sensitive mutation

Definition of done for Epic 7 backend

Epic 7 backend is done only when:

- EVV, geofence, signature-verification, missed-visit, exception, notification, and escalation models exist with clear lifecycle rules
- clock-in / clock-out APIs are available through real audited backend contracts
- GPS/time/device verification outcomes are evaluated and returned through structured contracts
- geofence tolerance behavior is configurable and test-covered
- missed-visit and exception workflows produce durable records and branch-aware notifications
- escalation or downstream order-creation hooks exist for exception-driven follow-up
- EVV readiness/compliance projections are available for frontend and later QA/revenue workflows
- branch-aware authorization and tenant isolation are enforced for EVV data and actions
- core Epic 7 workflows are regression-covered
