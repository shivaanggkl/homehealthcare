Epic 10 Backend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 10 from `Requirements.txt` into a backend delivery backlog.

Epic 10 title

QA / review workspace

Epic 10 feature scope from the requirements

- review queue
- returned-for-fix workflow
- approve / reject / request-signoff
- completeness checks
- missing-field detection
- exception queue
- audit trail of edits
- role-based review assignment

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 is complete and provides auth, session management, RBAC, tenancy, audit, branch-aware authorization, and notification foundations
- Epic 3 is complete and provides patient records and patient context needed for reviewable operational work
- Epic 5 is complete and provides visit, assignment, and schedule records that can generate QA-relevant reviewable work
- Epic 6 is complete and provides mobile execution outputs that may enter QA review after field completion
- Epic 7 is complete and provides EVV, missed-visit, exception, and signature verification outputs that QA may need to inspect but should not redefine
- Epic 8 is complete and provides documentation templates, visit documentation records, draft/submit behavior, printable summaries, and attachment linkage that form the primary Epic 10 review input
- Epic 9 is complete and provides secure communication and escalation context that Epic 10 may reference for reviewer coordination
- Epic 10 focuses on internal QA and review workflow, not the deeper clinical compliance dashboard from Epic 11, not incident/infection/wound longitudinal workflows from Epic 12, and not final billing or claims workflow from Epic 14
- Epic 10 should support work becoming reviewable, assigned, returned, corrected, approved, or rejected in a controlled lifecycle that is auditable and role-aware
- completeness and missing-field detection should rely on explicit backend rules and record projections rather than frontend-only heuristics

Scope rule

- Include only Epic 10 backend work
- Cover review queue domain models, completeness and exception evaluation, review assignment, return-for-fix, approve/reject/request-signoff actions, APIs, authorization, audit, and tests
- Do not include frontend-only tasks
- Do not pull full compliance checklist engine, physician-order workflows, billing workflows, or analytics backlog scope from later epics into Epic 10

BE10-01 — Define QA and review domain architecture
Related Epic 10 features: all
Current status: NOT STARTED

Story
As the platform architect, I need a coherent QA/review domain so reviewable work, completeness findings, assignment, and return loops follow one lifecycle and authorization model.

Recommended acceptance criteria

- Epic 10 entities are defined explicitly, including:
  - review work item
  - review assignment
  - review finding
  - completeness check result
  - missing-field result
  - review decision
  - return-for-fix event
  - signoff request
- reviewable source types are defined for at least:
  - visit documentation record
  - EVV verification or exception output
  - missed-visit record
  - visit-level execution artifact or summary
- lifecycle states are defined for at least:
  - pending review
  - assigned
  - in review
  - returned for fix
  - resubmitted
  - approved
  - rejected
  - signoff requested
  - signoff completed optional
- ownership, assignment, branch scope, and patient/visit/document linkage rules are documented
- audit expectations are defined for queue generation, assignment, decision, return-for-fix, signoff request, and completeness recalculation

Definition of done for Epic 10 backend

Epic 10 backend is done only when:

- review queue, assignment, completeness, missing-field, exception, decision, and signoff domain models exist with clear lifecycle rules
- review queue and exception queue APIs are available for real operational triage
- approve, reject, return-for-fix, assign, and signoff actions are implemented through audited APIs
- completeness and missing-field outcomes are available through structured backend contracts
- role-based review assignment and tenant or branch-aware authorization are enforced
- review history and audit context are queryable through backend APIs suitable for review UX
- returned-for-fix and resubmission behavior are explicitly modeled rather than treated as ad hoc notes
- core Epic 10 review flows are regression-covered
