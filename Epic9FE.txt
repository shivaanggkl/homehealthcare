Epic 9 Frontend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 9 from `Requirements.txt` into a frontend delivery backlog for messaging and coordination.

Epic 9 title

Messaging and coordination

Epic 9 feature scope from the requirements

- in-app secure messaging
- patient-level communication threads
- staff groups
- branch broadcasts
- visit-specific notes
- escalation tags
- read receipts
- task-linked discussion threads

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 frontend and backend are complete and provide auth, session, RBAC, protected-route, and audit-view patterns
- Epic 3 frontend and backend are complete and provide patient workspaces and patient-scoped operational context
- Epic 5 frontend and backend are complete and provide visit, schedule, and task-adjacent coordination entry points
- Epic 6 frontend and backend are complete and provide caregiver mobile messaging patterns that Epic 9 should generalize for web coordination workflows
- Epic 8 frontend and backend are complete and provide documentation/task completion context that can link into discussion threads
- Epic 9 frontend should support direct messaging, contextual operational communication, and branch announcements without turning the app into a generic external mail client
- Epic 9 should stop short of the QA queue and reviewer workflow from Epic 10
- secure coordination should remain privacy-aware and avoid exposing unnecessary patient or message metadata outside authorized contexts

Status legend

- BLOCKED: frontend should wait for the corresponding Epic 9 backend APIs

========================================================================
FE9-01 — Messaging information architecture and route model
Related Epic 9 features: all
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As a coordinator, admin, or caregiver, I want messaging and coordination routes to fit naturally into the app so conversations, broadcasts, and contextual notes are easy to find.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- protected messaging workspace route exists
- dedicated routes exist for:
  - inbox or thread list
  - thread detail
  - patient-linked discussion context
  - visit or task-linked discussion context
  - staff-group and broadcast admin surfaces
- unsupported or unauthorized messaging routes render controlled states
- unread and escalation state can be surfaced without exposing backend-only detail

FE9-10 — Shared send, read, and retry mutation UX
Related Epic 9 features: in-app secure messaging, read receipts
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As the frontend team, I want one mutation pattern for send, mark-read, broadcast, and escalation actions so messaging behavior stays predictable.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- send and update mutations use a consistent pending/success/failure pattern
- retry behavior is explicit for recoverable failures
- unread or read state does not use misleading optimistic behavior where data safety matters
- risky actions like broadcast and escalation are confirmed clearly
- failed message sends remain visible until resolved or retried where practical

Definition of done for Epic 9 frontend

Epic 9 frontend is done only when:

- messaging, contextual discussion, staff groups, and branch broadcasts are reachable through coherent protected routes
- inbox, thread detail, send, read, group, broadcast, and escalation flows use real backend APIs rather than placeholder local state
- patient, visit, and task-linked communication context is visible where authorized
- unread, read, and escalation state is shown clearly without exposing internal-only data
- coordinator-facing communication visibility exists for authorized users
- privacy-sensitive context and message metadata is minimized appropriately
- messaging screens are mobile-safe where staff use them
- core Epic 9 screens are regression-covered
