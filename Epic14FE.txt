Epic 14 Frontend Backlog
HomeHealthCare

Purpose

This document converts Epic 14 from `Requirements.txt` into a frontend delivery backlog for revenue readiness and basic financial ops.

Epic 14 title

Revenue readiness and basic financial ops

Epic 14 feature scope from the requirements

Early version

- visit completion validation
- signed visit validation
- exception flags
- export-ready invoices/payroll data
- authorization usage
- payer/service summary

Later version, intentionally out of scope for this backlog

- claim generation
- physician signature tracking
- denial worklists
- remittance posting

Planning assumptions

- Epic 1 frontend and backend are complete and provide auth, session, RBAC, protected-route, audit-view, and export-aware patterns
- Epic 3 frontend and backend are complete and provide patient and payer context that Epic 14 screens may reference
- Epic 5 frontend and backend are complete and provide visit and scheduling context that revenue-readiness screens will summarize
- Epic 7 frontend and backend are complete and provide EVV and signature context that contributes to signed-visit validation
- Epic 8 frontend and backend are complete and provide documentation state that contributes to visit completion validation
- Epic 10 and Epic 11 frontend and backend are complete and provide QA/compliance context that may influence readiness but should not redefine the revenue-readiness workflow
- Epic 14 frontend should support upstream readiness, blocker visibility, and export handoff, not full claims, denials, remittance, or physician-order workflow
- Epic 14 screens should stay privacy-aware and show only the minimum patient, payer, and visit detail needed for operational finance work

Status legend

- BLOCKED: frontend should wait for the corresponding Epic 14 backend APIs

FE14-01 — Revenue-readiness workspace information architecture and route model
Related Epic 14 features: all early-version features
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As a back-office user, owner, or operations lead, I want revenue-readiness routes to fit naturally into the app so blockers, exports, and usage context are easy to find.

Frontend acceptance criteria

- protected revenue-readiness workspace route exists
- dedicated routes exist for:
  - readiness list
  - readiness detail
  - exception queue or blocker list
  - payroll export view
  - invoice export view
  - authorization-usage summary
- unsupported or unauthorized Epic 14 routes render controlled states
- routes can link back into related patient, visit, documentation, QA, EVV, and payer context where authorized

FE14-09 — Shared export and readiness mutation UX
Related Epic 14 features: export-ready invoices/payroll data, exception flags
Backend status: BLOCKED

Story
As the frontend team, I want one mutation pattern for export generation and readiness refresh so Epic 14 behavior stays predictable and safe.

Frontend acceptance criteria

Definition of done for Epic 14 frontend

Epic 14 frontend is done only when:

- readiness, blocker, export, authorization-usage, and payer/service summary screens are reachable through coherent protected routes
- readiness, blocker, usage, payroll-export, invoice-export, and refresh flows use real backend APIs rather than placeholder local state
- ready/blocked/warning, missing-signature, completion, and usage-warning outcomes are shown clearly and consistently
- coordinator-facing revenue visibility exists for authorized users
- privacy-sensitive patient, payer, and visit context is minimized appropriately
- Epic 14 screens are usable on smaller operational devices
- core Epic 14 early-version screens are regression-covered
